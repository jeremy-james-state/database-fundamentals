---
title: "OR"
description: "Keeps a row when at least one combined WHERE condition is true."
---

Group `OR` with parentheses before you mix it with `AND`.

## Syntax

```sql
SELECT column
FROM table
WHERE condition OR condition;
```

## Arguments

<ParamField body="condition" type="boolean expression" required>
  At least one side must be true. Without parentheses, `AND` binds tighter than `OR` in SQLite.
</ParamField>

## Examples

<CodeGroup>
```sql Either year
SELECT title, author
FROM books
WHERE year = 2022
   OR year = 2023;
```
</CodeGroup>
