title: "Choose a content template"
description: "Pick a how-to, tutorial, explanation, or reference page, then copy it into a lesson."
hidden: true
---

Copy a template when you start a new page. Replace every `[placeholder]`. Remove `hidden: true`. Add the finished page to a visible navigation group in `docs.json`. Do not ship a template as a lesson.

| After the reader finishes | Use |
| --- | --- |
| They completed a specific task | [How-to](/templates/how-to) |
| They learned by doing a guided sequence | [Tutorial](/templates/tutorial) |
| They understand a concept | [Explanation](/templates/explanation) |
| They looked up a precise detail | [Reference](/templates/reference) |
